Your herd data.
One workspace.

Install once, sign into your smaXtec account, and collect your data and Excel reports. Python, browser tools and the required libraries are included.

1 · Install and open

  1. Download the SmaxTec Workbench Setup installer (.exe) from the location shared with you.
  2. Open it, keep Install for me only, and follow the installer.
  3. Open SmaxTec Workbench from the Start menu or your desktop shortcut.

This client-test build is unsigned. If your organisation blocks it, ask your IT contact to approve deployment. You do not need administrator rights for the default installation.

2 · Choose your cows and measurements

  1. In Direct data API, enter your authorised smaXtec email and password and connect.
  2. Select a farm, then select cows by clicking rows, selecting filtered cows, or pasting exact animal numbers.
  3. Set the From/To dates, add cows to the download queue and assign measurements to selected rows or all rows.
  4. Enter each cow's Study T0, or apply one T0 to the whole queue. T0 is the event or treatment time.

3 · Download and open your reports

  1. Choose an output folder, collection name and resolution. Native preserves individual readings.
  2. For the client-analysis baseline and follow-up fields, include at least 14 days before T0 and 20 days after T0 if you need the complete R20 window. Set your aligned-data window separately.
  3. Review the queue and download. Open your collection and workbooks from Results.

client-analysis.xlsx contains one cow/case per row plus aligned data and charts. Raw JSON, CSV files and provenance remain beside the reports. Missing source data stays blank. Daily resolution uses daily aggregates and cannot reconstruct six-hour activity windows.

When Lact/DIM are missing, Add Lact / DIM from website can add current website values to a new report. Those values describe the day they were read, not historical T0.

Updates, privacy and help

Close Workbench and run a newer installer to update. Uninstall through Windows Settings → Apps. Collections saved in your chosen output folders are preserved. Credentials are held for the session and released when you disconnect or close the application.

For help, send the person who supplied this installer your Workbench version, Windows version, the step you were attempting and the displayed error. Keep passwords and farm data out of ordinary messages.

Independent integration with smaXtec. Client-test preview; report definitions and results require client validation before clinical interpretation. An internet connection and an authorised smaXtec account are required. Legacy email access is optional and may require separate organisation approval.

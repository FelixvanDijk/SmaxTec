SmaxTec Workbench — desktop preview 0.2.1

1. Extract the complete ZIP into a folder on your computer.
2. Open SmaxTec Workbench.exe. Keep the _internal folder beside it.
3. Enter your smaXtec account details and choose Connect to smaXtec.
4. A browser opens and signs in. Complete any extra verification in that window.
5. Choose a discovered farm and enter one or more exact cow numbers.
6. Choose From/To dates, then review and confirm the request summary.
7. The app requests one measured-data export for each confirmed cow.
8. After a batch, its browser closes. A later confirmed batch reconnects automatically.
9. Choose Disconnect or close the app to end the in-memory account session.

No Python, editor, browser driver, or package installation is required.
Your website password is used for the current session and is not saved by the app.

This preview requests exports but does not collect the resulting emails yet.
Mailbox connection, Excel consolidation, and graphs are not included.

This is an unsigned development preview. The production release will have an
installer and will be verified on a computer without developer tools installed.
Do not move the executable out of its extracted folder.

This application is an independent integration project, not an official smaXtec app.
